# Cozy Budget

A cozy 2.5D house where every monthly bill is an object you can tap, and savings grow in a garden outside.

**Live demo:** https://invert-able.com/cozy.budget/

- A six-room cutaway house, yard and garden where every object can hold a bill. Over-budget bills get a pulsing red ring.
- Tap anything to see its bill, or to add one to an empty object. Lavender "+" coins suggest new categories.
- The Budget button shows a 100% bar of where take-home pay goes, plus an editor and CSV upload. Edits stay in your browser.
- Press Play month to watch the month unfold: paychecks stack the woodpile, spending feeds the stove, and at the end the gardener tends the savings that got money.
- Each garden row is a savings goal with 10 plants, one for every 10% of the goal.
- The emergency fund is an old wooden water tower that fills up as you save.

This is a demo with a made-up sample budget. No real financial data is used.
